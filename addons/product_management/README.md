# Product Management

Custom Odoo 19 module for the internal product management application.

The module provides the MVP features for internal product information
management:

- Product Management with category, brand, price, image, purpose, inventory
  quantity, product status, and category-specific specifications.
- Category Management with configurable dynamic fields and selection options.
- Product Discovery through product list search, filters, and group by.
- Dashboard with product counts, recently updated products, and stock attention lists.
- Product Comparison for comparing products in the same category.
- Basic Inventory with computed stock status.
- Account Management for Product Management Viewer/Admin users.

Reports, advanced inventory, and production deployment automation are handled
outside the current module scope or deferred to later epics.

Technical name: `product_management`.
